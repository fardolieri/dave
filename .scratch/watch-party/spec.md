# Watch parties

A YouTube card of the chat, watched together by friends in the call, in step (ADR 0012, CONTEXT.md "Watch party").

1. A friend in the call presses **Watch together** under a YouTube card: a party starts with that video, from the start,
   playing. One going on plays that card instead, for everyone in it.
2. It shows up for everyone, as a share does: a tile beside the shares with the card's thumbnail, its title and the faces
   of who is in it. A click puts a friend in the call in it, where it is; a visitor's tile says "Join to watch".
3. In it, the tile is YouTube's player. Anyone in it plays, pauses, seeks or changes the speed in YouTube's own controls,
   and everyone's player does the same. A player that drifts off is put back.
4. **Stop watching** takes a friend out; it goes on for the others. Leaving the call does too. It ends when nobody is in it.
5. Only videos of the chat: the party names its card, and an app follows only a party whose card it has.
